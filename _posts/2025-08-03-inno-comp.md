---
title: "We won Team and Individual Awards at the China International College Students’ Innovation Competition 2025！"
date: 2025-08-03
---

A student team supervised by our laboratory achieved outstanding results at the China International College Students’ Innovation Competition 2025 (Guangdong Division). The project *“Touching the Future: A VR Full-Body Motion Capture System”* was awarded the **Gold Award of the Guangdong Division** (also a Gold Award at the university level).

In addition, Prof.Huiping Zhuang was honored with the **Outstanding Innovation and Entrepreneurship Mentor Award**, presented by the Guangdong Provincial Department of Education.
