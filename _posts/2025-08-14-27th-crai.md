---
title: "Our team wins a first prize and a second prize at the 27th China Robot and Artificial Intelligence Competition!"
date: 2025-08-14
---

Our team achieved the First Prize and Second Prize at the 27th China Robot and Artificial Intelligence Competition. We developed an integrated application system based on the YOLOv8 model and Doubao large language model, leveraging the technical advantages of the embodied intelligence platform. In the retail scenario with specific venue settings and task requirements for the Robot Application Competition (Smart Retail) offline event, this system realizes functions such as commodity recognition to understand the relative positional relationship of goods, inferring buyers' needs through images and semantic reasoning, achieving precise picking, and ultimately completing autonomous settlement. It has successfully built an innovative smart retail service platform, demonstrating outstanding performance in practical retail service tasks.
